Privacy Policy for ClawBack: Kitty Screen Curtain

Effective Date: October 19, 2026

ClawBack: Kitty Screen Curtain ("ClawBack", "we", "our") is designed as a digital wellbeing tool to help you claw back your time from compulsive doomscrolling. We respect your right to privacy above all else.

Core Privacy Commitment: ClawBack does NOT collect, store, transmit, or share any personal information, keystrokes, messages, or device telemetry with any remote server or third party. The application runs 100% offline and locally on your device.

1. Accessibility Service API Declaration (Google Play Policy Compliance)

ClawBack requests permission to use Android's AccessibilityService API exclusively to deliver its primary screen-curtain functionality:

  • Scroll Event Detection: Detects vertical scroll gestures specifically within designated short-form media applications (Instagram Reels and YouTube Shorts).
  • Real-Time Scroll Measurement: Computes the distance scrolled locally to calculate view count (1 full screen height = 1 short-form video viewed).
  • Exclusions: Comments, YouTube long-form videos/Home feed, direct messages, and profile views are strictly ignored and never counted.
  • Zero Sensitive Data Capture: The service does NOT read passwords, keystrokes, text content, chat messages, credit card numbers, or any on-screen personal data.
  • No Network Transmission: The app possesses zero network permissions to communicate with servers. No accessibility telemetry leaves your phone.

2. Other Device Permissions

  • Display Over Other Apps (SYSTEM_ALERT_WINDOW): Allows the cute Kitty Curtain to slide down progressively from the top of the screen when your scroll threshold is approached.
  • Run at Startup (RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED): Used only to reschedule the midnight counter reset and maintain your chosen daily limits across device reboots.
  • Guardian / Parental Security Lock: Secures app limits and prevents unauthorized bypass or uninstallation without the Guardian PIN.

3. Local Data Storage

All app settings, selected kitty characters (Ginger, Midnight Tuxedo, etc.), daily scroll limits, and the hashed Guardian PIN are stored locally using Android SharedPreferences. Uninstalling the app completely purges all stored preferences.

4. Children's Privacy

ClawBack does not collect information from anyone, including children under 13. When used as parental control software, all configurations remain completely on the physical device under the parent or guardian's supervision.

5. Contact & Support

If you have questions regarding this Privacy Policy, please open an issue on our GitHub repository: https://github.com/raxblackwood/kitty-scroller.
