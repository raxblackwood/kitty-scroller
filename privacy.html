Privacy Policy for Clawse: Kitty Screen Curtain

Effective Date: October 22, 2026

Application: Clawse (com.clawse.app)

Clawse: Kitty Screen Curtain ("Clawse", "we", "our") is a digital wellbeing and mindful screen-time companion designed to help you claw back your focus from compulsive short-form doomscrolling. We respect your right to privacy above all else.

Core Privacy Guarantee: Clawse does NOT collect, store, transmit, or share any personal information, messages, keystrokes, contact lists, browsing history, or device identifiers with any remote server, advertising network, or third party. The application runs locally and offline on your device.

1. Accessibility Service API Declaration (Google Play Policy Compliance)

Clawse requires the Android AccessibilityService API exclusively for its core user-facing digital wellbeing features:

  • Scroll Detection in Monitored Apps: Detects vertical scroll gestures specifically within designated short-form video environments (Instagram Reels and YouTube Shorts).
  • Local Reel Counting: Measures vertical scroll distance locally to tally reels viewed against the daily boundary you have set.
  • Anti-Uninstall Friction & Guarding: Detects attempts to hastily uninstall or force-stop the app from system settings during a craving impulse, enforcing a mindful 60-second cooldown period or Guardian PIN verification.
  • Strict Scope & Privacy Protection:
    • The Accessibility Service ignores all comments, direct messages, passwords, text input, audio, cameras, search bars, and home feeds.
    • No keystrokes, personal messages, or screen text are ever recorded, analyzed, or stored.
    • The app does not possess network transmission servers for accessibility telemetry. No accessibility data ever leaves your device.

2. Other Permissions Used

  • Display Over Other Apps (SYSTEM_ALERT_WINDOW): Enables Kitty Curtain to slide down progressively over monitored short-form apps as your daily scroll limit is reached.
  • Run at Startup (RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED): Reschedules the local midnight counter reset and restores your chosen daily boundary after device reboots.
  • Foreground Service (FOREGROUND_SERVICE_SPECIAL_USE): Maintains reliable overlay rendering and local counter synchronization while actively guarding monitored apps.

3. In-App Purchases & Billing

Optional cosmetic kitty breeds (Tabby, Saturn, Rolex) and Guardian Mode features are processed securely through the official Google Play In-App Billing API. We do not process, store, or receive your credit card numbers, billing addresses, or payment credentials. All transactions are governed by Google Play's Terms of Service and Privacy Policy.

4. Data Storage & Deletion

All app settings, selected kitty breed, daily scroll limit counters, and the salted hash of your Guardian PIN are stored locally on your device in private Android SharedPreferences. Uninstalling Clawse permanently and immediately deletes all stored application data.

5. Children's Privacy

Clawse does not collect personal data from anyone, including children under 13. When utilized by families or parents as a screen-time boundary tool, all controls and PIN locks operate strictly locally on the physical device.

6. Security & Compliance

Because Clawse does not transmit user data across the Internet, your personal data is immune to external data breaches or server-side unauthorized access.
