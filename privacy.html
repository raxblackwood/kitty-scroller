Privacy Policy for Clawse: Kitty Screen Curtain

Effective Date: October 27, 2026

Application: Clawse (com.clawse.app)

Clawse: Kitty Screen Curtain ("Clawse", "we", "our") is a digital wellbeing and mindful screen-time companion designed to help you claw back your focus from compulsive short-form doomscrolling. We respect your right to privacy above all else.

Core Privacy Guarantee: Clawse does NOT collect, store, transmit, or share any personal information, messages, keystrokes, contact lists, browsing history, or scrolling activity. Everything Clawse does to limit doomscrolling happens locally on your device. The only data that ever leaves your phone is the small amount needed for the optional Guardian Alerts feature (see Section 4), and only if you choose to link two phones.

1. Accessibility Service API Declaration (Google Play Policy Compliance)

Clawse requires the Android AccessibilityService API exclusively for its core user-facing digital wellbeing features:

  • Scroll Detection in Monitored Apps: Detects vertical scroll gestures specifically within designated short-form video environments (Instagram Reels and YouTube Shorts).
  • Local Reel Counting: Measures vertical scroll distance locally to tally reels viewed against the daily boundary you have set.
  • Anti-Uninstall Friction & Guarding: Detects attempts to hastily uninstall or force-stop the app from system settings during a craving impulse, enforcing a mindful 60-second cooldown period or Guardian PIN verification.
  • Strict Scope & Privacy Protection:
    • The Accessibility Service ignores all comments, direct messages, passwords, text input, audio, cameras, search bars, and home feeds.
    • No keystrokes, personal messages, or screen text are ever recorded, analyzed, or stored.
    • No accessibility data ever leaves your device. Accessibility events are processed in memory and never uploaded.

2. Other Permissions Used

  • Display Over Other Apps (SYSTEM_ALERT_WINDOW): Enables Kitty Curtain to slide down progressively over monitored short-form apps as your daily scroll limit is reached.
  • Run at Startup (RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED): Reschedules the local midnight counter reset and restores your chosen daily boundary after device reboots.
  • Foreground Service (FOREGROUND_SERVICE_SPECIAL_USE): Maintains reliable overlay rendering and local counter synchronization while actively guarding monitored apps.

3. In-App Purchases & Billing

Optional cosmetic kitty breeds (Tabby, Saturn, Rolex) and Guardian Mode features are processed securely through the official Google Play In-App Billing API. We do not process, store, or receive your credit card numbers, billing addresses, or payment credentials. All transactions are governed by Google Play's Terms of Service and Privacy Policy.

4. Optional Guardian Alerts (Google Firebase)

Guardian Alerts let a parent or accountability partner receive a notification on their own phone when someone tries to remove Clawse from a linked phone, or when that phone stops checking in. This feature is off by default and is only used if both phones are linked with a pairing code. It uses Google Firebase (Authentication, Cloud Firestore and Cloud Messaging), operated by Google LLC, and a small Cloudflare Workers relay, operated by Cloudflare, Inc., that delivers the notifications. The relay does not store alert data.

When Guardian Alerts are in use, only the following data is sent to Firebase:

  • Anonymous app ID: a random identifier created by Firebase Anonymous Authentication. No name, email, phone number or account is required.
  • Pairing code and device label: the pairing code linking the two phones and the device name you enter (for example "Sam's phone") or the phone model.
  • Push notification token: a token for the guardian's phone so alerts can be delivered to it.
  • Check-in time: the time the protected phone last checked in (about every 15 minutes), used to warn the guardian if it goes silent.
  • Alert events: the type of event (for example an uninstall attempt) and a short message, forwarded to the guardian's phone and not stored.

Alert messages pass through the Cloudflare relay only to be forwarded to the guardian's phone. Clawse never sends scroll counts, app usage, screen content, location, contacts or any accessibility data to Firebase. This data is used only to deliver Guardian Alerts; it is not used for advertising, analytics or profiling, and is not sold or shared with anyone else. Data is encrypted in transit. Either phone can unlink at any time, which deletes the link and its data from the server; unused pairing codes are deleted automatically within 24 hours. You can read Google's privacy practices for Firebase at firebase.google.com/support/privacy.

If you prefer, Guardian Alerts can instead be sent to a webhook or ntfy.sh topic that you configure yourself; in that case the alert message is sent to the address you provide.

5. Data Storage & Deletion

All app settings, selected kitty breed, daily scroll limit counters, and the salted hash of your Guardian PIN are stored locally on your device in private Android SharedPreferences. Uninstalling Clawse permanently and immediately deletes all locally stored application data. Guardian Alert link data stored on Firebase is deleted when either phone unlinks; you can also request deletion through the contact details below.

6. Children's Privacy

Clawse does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. When a parent links a child's phone for Guardian Alerts, only the limited, non-identifying data listed in Section 4 is processed, solely to notify the parent. All other controls and PIN locks operate locally on the device.

7. Security & Compliance

Everything except Guardian Alerts stays on your device and is never transmitted. Guardian Alert data is protected by Firebase security rules so that only the two linked phones can access their own link, and it is encrypted in transit.
